Saad Kabir Uddin

I am a Software Engineer with experience developing enterprise and core banking applications, alongside an academic research background in machine learning and computer vision.

After completing my undergraduate degree in Computer Science, I chose to pursue a career in industry to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world software engineering. Working on complex, security-sensitive applications has given me practical insight into the challenges of building dependable systems and motivated me to explore research in software security, AI/LLM security and privacy, and cyber-physical systems (CPS). I am particularly interested in improving the security, safety, and reliability of software and AI-enabled systems, including autonomous systems and UAVs. I am also interested in how these guarantees hold further down the stack, in operating systems, embedded platforms, and real-time systems.

Research Interests

My research interests span software security, AI/LLM security and privacy, and systems security. I am particularly interested in vulnerability analysis, software supply chain security, and the security and privacy of LLM agents, including permissions, provenance, and isolation. I am also interested in how security and reliability guarantees hold further down the stack, in operating systems, embedded platforms, and real-time and cyber-physical systems. More broadly, I am interested in software engineering, including software testing and AI-assisted software development.
